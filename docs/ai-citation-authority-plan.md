# SoltaSite AI Citation and Authority Plan

**Status:** Active  
**Date:** 2026-11-01  
**Governing docs:** `docs/seo-operating-plan.md`, `docs/search-aeo-geo-measurement.md`

## Goal

Make SoltaSite useful and trustworthy enough to be cited in traditional search, AI answers, actor-industry articles, and professional resource pages.

This is not a backlink-volume project.

The priority order is:

1. crawlable and indexable public pages,
2. clear answer-first content,
3. primary-source evidence on factual guides,
4. consistent SoltaSite entity information,
5. real third-party mentions,
6. original first-party research worth citing.

## Current technical state

- Public pages are crawlable under the wildcard robots rule.
- OAI-SearchBot is not blocked by robots.txt.
- Bing Webmaster Tools is connected.
- Bing AI Grounding should remain allowed.
- Search attribution tracks AI referrals from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot, Claude and Gemini.
- Three core guides include visible primary-source references and Article citation metadata.

## On-site citation rule

Use citations where a factual claim benefits from verification.

Prefer:
- official platform documentation,
- search-engine documentation,
- government/institutional sources,
- primary industry sources.

Do not add citations to obvious SoltaSite product facts that are controlled by SoltaSite itself.

Do not create giant bibliography sections just to increase link count.

## External authority lanes

### 1. Daily Actor

Contributor page:
https://www.dailyactor.com/contribute/

Why it is worth testing:
- actor-specific audience,
- explicitly accepts columns/tips/advice,
- accepted columns can include a link to the contributor's web page.

Pitch topics should be educational, not a SoltaSite advertisement.

Potential pitch:
**What an actor website should do after someone already found you on a casting platform**

The article can naturally reference SoltaSite research or a relevant SoltaSite guide when it genuinely supports a point.

### 2. Actor Aesthetic

Guest-article contact:
https://maggie-bera-9z9k.squarespace.com/contact

Why it is worth testing:
- actor-focused career/resource audience,
- explicitly accepts guest-article pitches.

Potential pitch:
**What to put on an actor website and what to leave off**

Avoid duplicating the SoltaSite guide word-for-word. Add a distinct angle and use SoltaSite as the author/business source.

### 3. Backstage

Backstage already publishes actor website guidance and is a strong topical authority.

This is an earned editorial target, not a guest-post-volume target.

Useful future angles:
- original Actor Website Benchmark data,
- expert commentary on actor-site design,
- current data about common actor-website mistakes.

Do not pitch a generic “why actors need websites” article. They already have that coverage.

### 4. Casting Networks

Casting Networks publishes actor career guidance and maintains authoritative documentation about talent profiles and assets.

Treat as a future editorial/data-partnership target.

Best pitch only after SoltaSite has:
- real customers,
- original benchmark data,
- a useful first-party finding.

### 5. Acting coaches, headshot photographers and reel editors

These are good resource-page and collaboration targets when the relationship is real.

Useful collaboration formats:
- co-written checklist,
- resource roundup,
- expert quote exchange,
- webinar/workshop,
- actor marketing resource page.

Do not mass-email hundreds of businesses with identical backlink requests.

### 6. Customer and collaborator mentions

A real actor, photographer, coach, editor or representative may mention SoltaSite when the relationship naturally calls for it.

Do not require keyword-rich footer backlinks on customer sites.

If a customer wants to credit the builder, use a normal brand mention such as “Website by Solta Site.”

## Citation magnet

The strongest future asset is the **Actor Website Benchmark / State of Actor Websites** already defined in the SEO operating plan.

Target methodology:
- review 50 to 100 public actor websites,
- publish the sampling method,
- report aggregate findings,
- distinguish observations from recommendations,
- date the dataset,
- publish charts/tables,
- cite source categories without exposing sensitive information.

Potential findings:
- percentage using a custom domain,
- percentage with reel above the fold,
- percentage with downloadable résumé,
- percentage with current representation/contact,
- percentage with IMDb/casting links,
- mobile performance,
- common page counts,
- maintenance/freshness signals.

This is much more citeable than another generic advice article.

## Outreach rules

Never:
- buy backlinks,
- use PBNs,
- use mass guest-post networks,
- pay for “dofollow” packages,
- create fake author profiles,
- trade links at scale,
- claim data we did not collect.

Prefer:
- useful expert contribution,
- original research,
- genuine collaboration,
- interviews,
- resource-page inclusion,
- customer proof.

## Measurement

Track:
- referring domains,
- branded mentions,
- organic referral traffic,
- AI referral traffic,
- Bing AI citations,
- grounding queries,
- cited pages,
- citation share,
- conversions from AI and referral sources.

A mention without a link can still be useful for entity recognition. A relevant editorial link is stronger than dozens of unrelated directory links.
