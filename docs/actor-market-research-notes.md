## Why this research is being kept

Research into Las Vegas, Oklahoma City, Albuquerque, Austin, and Atlanta surfaced recurring actor questions that are more useful as national guides than as location landing pages.

The research may support future editorial pages only after a distinct-intent and SERP review.

## Reusable findings

### Local hire

Across multiple production markets, casting and film-office materials use local-hire or local-resident concepts. This may support a national guide covering:

- what “local hire” means for an actor,
- how casting notices phrase local-hire requirements,
- what an actor should and should not claim about location,
- where location belongs on an actor website, résumé, or casting profile,
- how to handle more than one legitimate working market.

**Possible future intent:** local hire actor / what does local hire mean acting.

Do not publish until current SERPs and query demand are reviewed.

### Self-tapes and what happens next

The market research also reinforced how common self-tape workflows are across acting markets. A useful national guide could address:

- what casting may look at after a self-tape,
- why a current headshot and reel still matter,
- when someone may search the actor's name,
- how résumé, credits, representation, and contact information should be easy to verify,
- what an actor website can and cannot do in that process.

**Possible future intent:** what casting directors look at after self tape / actor self tape website.

Do not claim that a website causes callbacks or bookings.

## Market notes retained for future first-party research

### Las Vegas / Nevada

Research sources included Film Nevada, the Nevada Production Directory, UNLV Stage and Screen Acting, Nevada Conservatory Theatre audition information, and Las Vegas casting resources. The market includes a mix of screen, commercial, stage, live-entertainment, independent, student, and promotional work.

### Oklahoma City / Oklahoma

Research identified a growing production environment, state/local incentive activity, and casting/production resources. This may be useful later when SoltaSite has real Oklahoma customers or first-party data.

### Albuquerque / New Mexico

Research identified a mature New Mexico production ecosystem, resident-cast considerations, Albuquerque/Santa Fe overlap, and strong film/TV infrastructure.

### Austin / Texas

Research identified a broad creative market spanning film, commercial, voiceover, independent, and branded work, alongside Texas production incentives and resident-cast considerations.

### Atlanta / Georgia

Research identified a mature and competitive film/TV market with a large working-actor population and heavier competition than the emerging markets reviewed.
