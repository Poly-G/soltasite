## 5. Why Las Vegas is genuinely distinct

The city page has enough market-specific information to earn its own URL.

### A. Nevada has an active film-production infrastructure

Film Nevada states that it supports hundreds of productions annually and maintains crew, services, talent, stage/studio and location resources for productions filming in Nevada.

Source:
https://film.nv.gov/

### B. The production ecosystem explicitly includes local talent

Film Nevada's filming resources include talent agencies, casting directors and background agencies as part of Nevada's production infrastructure.

Source:
https://film.nv.gov/filming-in-nevada/

### C. Nevada's production directory is relevant to professional identity

Film Nevada says its production directory is a key resource for industry professionals filming in Nevada. Approved listings can include:
- profile image,
- experience,
- credits,
- resume,
- website,
- video reel,
- social links.

That directly supports the usefulness of a professional web presence for working Nevada performers.

Source:
https://film.nv.gov/about-us/film-nevadas-directories/

### D. Las Vegas is close to Los Angeles without being Los Angeles

Film Nevada describes Las Vegas as roughly an hour's flight from Los Angeles, with Harry Reid International Airport serving more than 150 nonstop destinations.

This supports a useful site-design question for actors who identify with more than one working market.

Source:
https://film.nv.gov/filming-in-nevada/

### E. The local acting ecosystem is broader than feature film

Current Las Vegas casting data in October 2026 shows a mix including:
- film and scripted projects,
- commercials,
- student films,
- live events,
- promotional/event work,
- social/digital content.

Current sources reviewed:
- Backstage Las Vegas casting
- Central Casting Vegas
- HeyBooker Las Vegas
- VegasAuditions.com

This means the page should not treat a “Las Vegas actor” as only a film/TV performer.

### F. Las Vegas has a real training / emerging-talent pipeline

UNLV runs a Stage and Screen Acting program covering stage, film and television performance. Nevada Conservatory Theatre auditions are also open beyond theatre majors and include community participation.

Sources:
https://www.unlv.edu/theatre/stage-and-screen
https://callboard.sites.unlv.edu/auditions/

UNLV's School of Cinematic Arts also runs student-created film production and local creative initiatives, adding to the early-career production ecosystem.

Sources:
https://www.unlv.edu/unit/cinematic-arts
https://www.unlv.edu/announcement/unlv-school-cinematic-arts/unlv-school-cinematic-arts-student-created-short-domino
https://www.unlv.edu/announcement/college-fine-arts/unlv-film-alumna-brings-music-video-lab-back-las-vegas

## 6. Page-specific audience

The page should speak primarily to:

1. Las Vegas actors with current headshots/reel/resume who need a single professional link.
2. Actors doing a mixture of film/TV, commercial, live entertainment or branded work.
3. Actors building credits through local/student/independent work.
4. Las Vegas actors who also identify with or submit in another market such as Los Angeles.
5. Actors without representation who still need a safe professional contact route.
6. Represented actors who need their rep contact and materials presented cleanly.

Do not claim that all Las Vegas actors work in multiple markets.

Use conditional language such as:

> If you work in both Las Vegas and Los Angeles, your site can show both markets without making the page confusing.
