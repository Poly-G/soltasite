# SoltaSite SEO Operating Plan

**Status:** Governing plan  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-10-31  
**Scope:** SoltaSite actor vertical only  
**Primary business goal:** Generate qualified organic traffic that converts into $499 done-for-you actor website purchases.

---

## 1. SEO thesis

SoltaSite will not win by publishing the most actor-website articles.

It will win by owning a small set of high-intent searches with pages that are:
- clearly separated by intent,
- useful enough to deserve indexation,
- commercially connected to the $499 offer,
- server-rendered and technically clean,
- backed by first-party product facts, real examples and eventually original data,
- maintained instead of abandoned.

The core positioning is:

> **Done-for-you actor websites for $499 once. Built around the performer, live on their own domain, without a monthly SoltaSite hosting fee.**

SoltaSite is **not** positioned as:
- a casting platform,
- a DIY website builder,
- a replacement for Actors Access / Casting Networks / IMDb,
- a promise of auditions, bookings, rankings or leads.

The SEO strategy must preserve that distinction.

---

## 2. Non-negotiable operating rules

1. **No new indexable URL without a distinct search intent.**
2. **One primary query family per page.**
3. **Do not create keyword-variant pages** for actress/actor, city names, plural/singular, or similar synonyms.
4. **No doorway/location pages** unless SoltaSite later has a genuinely different local product or local dataset.
5. **No programmatic SEO** without a unique dataset behind every page.
6. **No “best” comparison page without documented methodology and first-party research.**
7. **No unsupported outcome claims** about casting, bookings, traffic, rankings or income.
8. **No fake reviews, fake customers, fake case studies or fake usage data.**
9. **Publish fewer, stronger pages.** Refresh and consolidate before adding net-new URLs.
10. **Every indexable page must be server-rendered/static and accessible in HTML without client-side JavaScript.**
11. **Every supporting page must link contextually toward a commercial page.**
12. **Every commercial page must link to evidence:** examples, scope, pricing or process.
13. **No SEO page exists only to capture traffic.** It must help a real actor make a decision.
14. **Search Console data overrides assumptions.** Once query data exists, the plan is revised from evidence.

## 3. Market position

### SoltaSite's search-space competitors

The current actor-website SERP contains four major groups:

1. **Actor-specific builders**  
   Example: Web For Actors. Strong on actor-specific functionality, self-service management, imported credits and recurring subscriptions.

2. **General website builders**  
   Squarespace, Wix, Webflow, WordPress, Carrd and similar tools. Strong on DIY control and broad functionality.

3. **Done-for-you actor designers**  
   Higher-touch custom services. Often higher upfront project costs and sometimes ongoing hosting/maintenance.

4. **Editorial guides / affiliate roundups**  
   Actor website examples, builder comparisons and career-advice articles.

### SoltaSite's wedge

SoltaSite should not try to out-feature a builder or out-customize a $4,000+ studio.

Its wedge is:

- actor-specific,
- done for you,
- human-built,
- fixed $499 price,
- fast turnaround,
- own domain,
- no monthly SoltaSite hosting fee,
- two change rounds,
- two updates per year included.

That is the message commercial SEO pages should reinforce.

## 5A. Geographic expansion strategy

SoltaSite remains an **online-only business**. We will not create or maintain a Google Business Profile unless the operating model later changes to genuine in-person customer service that satisfies Google's eligibility rules.

Local SEO will therefore focus on **organic search**, not Google Maps / Local Pack rankings.

### Target markets

The approved expansion sequence is:

1. **Las Vegas, Nevada**
2. **Oklahoma City, Oklahoma**
3. **Albuquerque, New Mexico**
4. **Austin, Texas**
5. **Atlanta, Georgia**

These markets are selected because they combine active or growing production ecosystems with meaningful populations of working and developing actors. Current official sources show active production support and/or incentives in Nevada, Oklahoma, New Mexico, Texas/Austin and Georgia.

### Local-page rule

A city page is allowed only when it contains genuinely city-specific value.

A valid local page must include several of the following:

- local actor / production context,
- market-specific guidance,
- local casting / production resources,
- local headshot, reel, coaching or industry resources where useful,
- how actors in that market commonly overlap with nearby production markets,
- local examples or customers once available,
- unique internal links and copy based on the city's real ecosystem.

A local page must **not** be a rewritten national page with only the city name changed.

### Planned local URLs

- `/las-vegas-actor-website-design`
- `/oklahoma-city-actor-website-design`
- `/albuquerque-actor-website-design`
- `/austin-actor-website-design`
- `/atlanta-actor-website-design`

### Rollout rule

Do not publish all five at once.

Launch sequentially:

**Las Vegas → Oklahoma City → Albuquerque → Austin → Atlanta**

Each subsequent market opens only after the previous market page has:

- been indexed,
- accumulated initial Search Console impressions,
- passed a duplicate-content / doorway-page review,
- shown that the local template is producing unique content rather than thin variants.

### Primary local query families

Each page should own its city-modified commercial intent, for example:

- actor website design Las Vegas
- actor websites Las Vegas
- website design for actors Las Vegas
- Las Vegas actor website designer

Do **not** create separate pages for each keyword variation.

### Online-only positioning

Because SoltaSite remains online:

- do not claim a local office,
- do not claim in-person service,
- do not publish fake local addresses,
- do not use coworking or virtual-office addresses for local-search manipulation,
- do not imply membership in a local industry organization unless true.

The page may accurately say SoltaSite serves actors in that market online.

### Geographic expansion gate

A city page must earn its own existence.

Before publishing a market page, document:

1. the target search intent,
2. current local SERP competition,
3. the city's actor / production ecosystem,
4. at least three pieces of city-specific information the national page does not contain,
5. the internal-link path to the national commercial page,
6. how the page will be materially different from the previous city page.

## 10. Technical SEO plan

### Must remain true

- Next.js server/static rendering for indexable content.
- Canonical URLs use `https://soltasite.com`.
- `www.soltasite.com` redirects permanently to the apex.
- Public indexable pages return 200.
- Preview/deployment URLs remain noindex.
- APIs are excluded from crawling.
- Fictional demo pages remain noindex.
- Utility/private flows remain noindex.
- Sitemap contains only canonical, indexable URLs.
- Important links exist in rendered HTML.

### Change from current implementation

**`/start` should remain noindex and should not be in the XML sitemap even after checkout is live.**

The checkout page is a conversion utility, not a search landing page. Search traffic should enter through the homepage or commercial pages.

### Core Web Vitals targets

At p75 field data:
- LCP ≤ 2.5s
- INP ≤ 200ms
- CLS ≤ 0.1

Treat these primarily as UX/conversion requirements.

### Structured data

Use schema for entity clarity and eligible search features, not as a ranking trick.

Current / planned:
- Organization on the site.
- Service on commercial service pages.
- Article on editorial guides.
- BreadcrumbList once breadcrumb navigation is implemented.
- Offer only when live commercial availability is true and the visible page matches it.

Do not depend on FAQ schema for rich results.

## 13. Decision rules from Search Console

### If a page is not indexed after 30 days

Check:
- duplicate intent,
- thin/repetitive content,
- internal-link depth,
- canonical mismatch,
- sitemap inclusion,
- technical response,
- whether the page genuinely deserves to exist.

Do not create more pages to compensate.

### If two SoltaSite pages trade rankings for the same query

Run a cannibalization review.

Pick one owner page and:
- consolidate overlapping content,
- adjust title/H1/internal anchors,
- redirect the weaker page if intents are not truly distinct.

### If a page gets impressions but poor CTR

Test title/meta copy before changing the entire page.

### If a page ranks roughly positions 4–20

Prioritize:
- stronger evidence,
- clearer answer-first sections,
- improved internal links,
- useful original examples/data,
- relevant external mentions.

### If a page has no meaningful impressions after ~90 days

If it is not strategically required:
- consolidate,
- redirect,
- noindex,
- or remove.

## 18. Execution roadmap

### Phase 0 — Reconcile what exists

**Do this before creating another SEO page.**

1. Freeze net-new SEO URLs.
2. Merge comparison page into `/do-actors-need-a-website`.
3. Add permanent redirect (308 in Next.js).
4. Remove merged URL from sitemap.
5. Remove `/start` from sitemap permanently.
6. Audit every surviving page for:
   - unique intent,
   - title/H1 overlap,
   - duplicated sections,
   - unsupported claims,
   - internal links,
   - CTA fit.
7. Reconcile the working branch with the intended long-term production branch.
8. Verify all intended index pages render correctly on the canonical domain.

**Gate:** no duplicate intent, no orphan pages, sitemap equals indexable canonical set.

---

### Phase 1 — Indexation foundation

1. Complete Google Search Console verification.
2. Submit sitemap.
3. Request indexing for homepage + main money/proof pages.
4. Set up Bing Webmaster Tools.
5. Add IndexNow.
6. Run production crawl and Lighthouse checks.
7. Verify robots/canonicals on the public domain.
8. Add analytics conversion events.

**Gate:** all intended SEO URLs discoverable, canonical and technically indexable.

---

### Phase 2 — Wait for query evidence

For the first 30–60 days:
- do not spray new pages,
- watch impressions and query ownership,
- improve titles/meta/internal links,
- fix indexation,
- gather real customer questions.

Refresh existing pages before adding new ones.

**Gate:** enough Search Console data to identify real query gaps.

---

### Phase 3 — Proof and authority

As real customers launch:
- add real examples,
- create honest case-study/proof content where enough detail exists,
- build industry partnerships,
- pursue relevant brand mentions.

Begin collecting methodology/data for the Actor Website Benchmark.

---

### Phase 4 — Evidence-led expansion

Only expand from actual data.

Possible next URLs:
- actor domain guide,
- child actor website guide,
- reel-on-website guide,
- bio/about guidance.

No URL is approved solely because a keyword tool lists volume.

---

### Phase 5 — New performer verticals

Do not mix musicians/models/dancers into the actor cluster prematurely.

When actor SEO + production is proven:
- create a separate vertical architecture,
- define each vertical's unique materials and search intent,
- reuse technical infrastructure but not thinly rewritten copy.

---

## 19. Publishing cadence

Until SoltaSite has meaningful search data:

**0 net-new SEO pages after Phase 0 cleanup.**

After data exists:

**Maximum default cadence: one genuinely new indexable page every 2–4 weeks**, and only when it passes the intent/information-gain gate.

Publishing speed is never the KPI.

## 20. What “done” looks like for v1 SEO

SoltaSite v1 SEO is complete when:

- the surviving page map is reconciled,
- comparison content is consolidated,
- every indexed URL has a unique job,
- sitemap contains only canonical indexable URLs,
- Search Console and Bing are connected,
- core pages are indexed,
- analytics can attribute organic starts/purchases,
- technical performance is healthy,
- no new content is being created without Search Console evidence or a clear commercial reason.

At that point, SEO becomes an operating loop:

> **Measure → refresh → consolidate → strengthen → expand only when evidence says to.**
