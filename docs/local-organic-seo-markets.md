# SoltaSite Local Organic SEO Market Plan

**Status:** Approved direction  
**Date:** 2026-10-31  
**Business model:** Online-only  
**Google Business Profile:** Not part of the strategy unless the business later adds genuine in-person service that meets Google's eligibility requirements.

## Approved market order

1. Las Vegas, Nevada
2. Oklahoma City, Oklahoma
3. Albuquerque, New Mexico
4. Austin, Texas
5. Atlanta, Georgia

## Why these markets

### Las Vegas

SoltaSite begins here because it is the home market and gives the brand the strongest legitimate local relevance without pretending to maintain a local office or in-person operation.

Nevada maintains active production support, crew/service directories and film-industry infrastructure. Las Vegas also sits close to Los Angeles geographically and by air, which makes it relevant to actors who may work across both markets.

**Planned URL:** `/las-vegas-actor-website-design`

**Primary intent:** actor website design Las Vegas

**Unique angles to research/use:**
- Las Vegas actors who also submit/work in Los Angeles
- film/TV plus commercial/live-entertainment crossover
- Nevada production/casting resources
- local headshot/reel/coaching ecosystem
- how to present a multi-market actor site when the actor works in Vegas + LA

## Local organic page template

Each city page must answer a different local problem while sharing only the product facts that are necessarily common.

### Required sections

1. **Answer-first local hero**
   - state that SoltaSite serves actors in the city online
   - no false local-office or in-person claims

2. **Why the city is a distinct acting market**
   - current, sourced production/casting context
   - concise; not tourism filler

3. **What actors in that market need from a website**
   - market-specific guidance

4. **Local resources**
   - official film office / casting / production resources
   - carefully selected relevant industry resources
   - no paid-link directory dump

5. **How SoltaSite fits**
   - $499 once
   - done for you
   - own domain
   - no monthly SoltaSite hosting fee
   - same published scope as national offer

6. **Examples / proof**
   - real examples as they become available
   - fictional examples remain clearly labeled

7. **National-service bridge**
   - link to `/actor-website-design`
   - link to examples/cost/checklist only where useful

## Doorway-page prevention test

Before publishing any local page, compare it with the previously published city page.

The new page FAILS if:
- most paragraphs are identical except the city/state name,
- the local industry section could be swapped into another city's page unchanged,
- the same resource list is reused,
- the same headings and examples are reused without a city-specific reason,
- it offers no distinct information gain.

A passing page should still be useful to a local actor even if the reader already knows SoltaSite's national offer.

## Rollout gates

### Market 1 — Las Vegas

Publish first.

Do not open Oklahoma City until:
- Las Vegas page is indexed,
- Search Console shows initial impressions or confirms stable indexation,
- no doorway/thin-content issue is visible,
- the page has at least one local authority/mention/outreach path identified.

### Market 2 — Oklahoma City

Same gate before Albuquerque.

### Market 3 — Albuquerque

Same gate before Austin.

### Market 4 — Austin

Same gate before Atlanta.

### Market 5 — Atlanta

Launch with the strongest proof and most mature local-page format because competition is expected to be higher.

## Google Business Profile rule

SoltaSite remains online-only.

Therefore:
- do not create a Google Business Profile,
- do not use a home address to manufacture local-pack eligibility,
- do not use virtual offices, coworking addresses or mailbox locations,
- do not create separate fake local profiles for target cities.

If SoltaSite later begins genuine in-person service, GBP eligibility can be reevaluated from Google's then-current rules.

## Measurement by city

Track each local page separately in Search Console.

Primary metrics:
- indexed/not indexed
- impressions for city-modified queries
- clicks
- CTR
- average position
- conversion to Start clicks
- organic purchase attribution once checkout is live

Secondary signals:
- backlinks/mentions from local acting or production resources
- branded searches containing SoltaSite + city
- customer inquiries mentioning the city

## Expansion rule

The approved five markets are a roadmap, not permission to publish five thin pages simultaneously.

The order is fixed unless Search Console or customer data gives a strong reason to revise it:

**Las Vegas → Oklahoma City → Albuquerque → Austin → Atlanta**
